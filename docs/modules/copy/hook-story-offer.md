# Hook-Story-Offer (HSO) — Russell Brunson

> Short-form copy framework from DotCom Secrets.
> Use for: social media ads, email, short video scripts, landing page headlines.
> Works WITHOUT a personal founder story — uses customer perspective + brand differentiation instead.

---

## Framework Structure

### 1. HOOK (Stop the scroll)

Its ONLY job is to make the reader stop and pay attention. It does NOT sell the product yet.

**Hook types:**
- **Curiosity:** Tease an outcome without revealing everything
- **Empathy:** Call out a specific pain point or fear
- **Promise:** State a clear benefit they will achieve
- **Pattern interrupt:** Say something unexpected that breaks their scrolling

---

### 2. STORY (Build trust & connection)

Share a relatable narrative that demonstrates empathy. Position the customer as the hero, the product as the guide.

**Weave in rapport elements where they fit naturally:**
- **"It's not your fault"** — Relieve blame. They failed because of bad info/tools, not inability.
- **Confirm suspicions** — Validate what they already suspect about the industry.
- **Throw rocks** — Criticize the old way / the common enemy.

**Story sources (no founder backstory needed):**
- Customer-perspective scenario ("Imagine you...")
- Industry-level problem narrative ("Most [industry] companies do X...")
- Before/after contrast using brand's differentiator

**The story should lead naturally to: "There IS a better way."**

---

### 3. OFFER (Make it irresistible)

Present your product/service as the logical solution to the problem highlighted in the story.

**Key elements:**
- Value clearly stated (why it's a "no-brainer")
- Clear call-to-action (CTA)
- Urgency, scarcity, or guarantee to reduce risk

---

## Diagnostic: If an ad isn't working

Russell Brunson teaches: it is ALWAYS because of one of these three elements.

| Problem | Symptom | Fix |
|---------|---------|-----|
| Weak Hook | Low reach / no clicks | Test different headlines, images, opening lines |
| Boring Story | Clicks but no engagement | Make it more relatable, add conflict, weave in rapport |
| Weak Offer | Engagement but no conversions | Strengthen value, add guarantee, clarify CTA |

## Example: Roofing Services

**HOOK:**
3 takofferter. 3 helt olika priser. Ingen som visar dig vad som faktiskt är fel. Känns det bekant?

**STORY:**
De flesta husägare väljer takfirma baserat på pris — för det finns inget annat att gå på. Ingen visar dig taket. Ingen förklarar vad som behöver göras. Du får en siffra och förväntas säga ja.

Det är inte ditt fel att du inte vet vem du kan lita på. Branschen har gjort det omöjligt att jämföra.

[Företagsnamn] gör det annorlunda. Vi filmar hela inspektionen med drönare och visar dig exakt vad som behöver åtgärdas — innan du betalar en krona.

**OFFER:**
Boka en gratis drönare-inspektion. Videorapport inom 24h — oavsett om du väljer oss eller inte.

👉 Boka på [länk] — begränsat antal tider i juni.
